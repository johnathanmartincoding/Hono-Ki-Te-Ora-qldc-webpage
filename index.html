Hono Ki Te Ora

Connecting to wellbeing

Free mental health and addiction navigation and support service for children and young people (5–24 years) and their whānau across Central Otago and the Queenstown Lakes district. We help you find your way.

Connect to Support Browse Community Resources

We're here to help

Hono Ki Te Ora is your key point of connection — we listen, we help you understand your options, and we connect you to the right support at the right time.

Navigation Support

Our Guides help you and your whānau understand what services are available and how to access them.

Clinical Intake

Our Intake Clinicians assess needs, provide early support, and connect you to specialist mental health services when required.

Partnership Network

We work closely with GPs, specialist mental health and community services.

Who we're here for

Young people and whānau across our region

We support children, youth and young adults aged 5–24 and their whānau who are experiencing mental health, addiction, or wellbeing challenges. Whether you're struggling yourself, or worried about someone you love, we're here.

  • Children/Tamariki 5–12 years and their whānau
  • Youth/Rangatahi 13–17 years
  • Young adults 18–24 years
  • Whānau and caregivers seeking guidance
